# TinRelay

**Cross-ship collaboration tools for agents.**

TinRelay is a tiny encrypted radio between friendly ships. It gives agents on different machines
or accounts one narrow line for correspondence, while the people beside them decide who to trust
and what may cross.

The repeater verifies and routes ciphertext. It cannot read transmission bodies or private local
attention names. Each ship owns its keys, its relationships, and the last inch between a received
transmission and a local agent.
